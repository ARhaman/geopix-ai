About

GeoPix AI turns complex reservoir data into practical outputs that modelers can use faster, with less manual interpretation.

Problem

Conventional geomodeling is slow, manual, and often struggles to preserve spatial continuity in heterogeneous reservoirs.

Solution

Our generative AI framework learns relationships between geological properties and produces consistent translated models.

Outcome

Shorter modeling cycles, reproducible workflows, and a path to scalable subsurface intelligence products.

Product

A modular platform for property-to-property translation and reconstruction.

Pix2Geomodel Engine

  • Image-to-image translation for reservoir properties
  • Masked property reconstruction
  • Structured outputs for downstream interpretation
  • Designed around a U-Net generator and PatchGAN discriminator

GeoPix Platform

  • Dataset organization and preprocessing
  • Model inference and batch processing
  • Workflow automation for subsurface teams
  • Extensible to geothermal and CCS projects

How it works

Simple, repeatable workflow from source data to modeled outputs.

01

Acquire reservoir data

Import facies and petrophysical grids, logs, or interpreted model slices.

02

Preprocess and augment

Normalize, resize, and expand training samples while preserving geological structure.

03

Train the model

Fit the Pix2Geomodel cGAN to learn cross-property relationships and reconstruction patterns.

04

Generate outputs

Predict missing properties or translate from one reservoir property to another.
