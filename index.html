How it works

Pix2Geomodel, our peer-reviewed, patent-pending engine, turns facies into continuous property models, and properties back into facies, from the data you already have.

  1. 01LearnTrains on the intervals you have cored and logged.
  2. 02TranslateFacies to properties, and properties back to facies.
  3. 03DecideYour geologists review and sign off. Hours, not days.
  • Less repetitive modellingProperty population, automated.
  • Faster reservoir characterizationUpdate a model the same day.
  • Experts focus on decisionsTime goes to interpretation.
