About us

Where Geoscience Meets Generative AI.

GeoPix AI is a Saudi-based deep-tech startup developing generative AI solutions for subsurface property modelling. Built on Pix2Geomodel research, the company combines expertise in geology, geophysics, artificial intelligence, and reservoir characterization.

Our Mission

Accelerate subsurface modelling through innovative AI technology while keeping geological expertise at the center of interpretation and decision-making.

Our Vision

Make AI-powered subsurface modelling more efficient, accessible, and scalable across energy and environmental applications.
